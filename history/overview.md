## What the business represented

Flamingo Byte was Spencer Armand's independent consulting and digital-services venture. Recovered contemporaneous planning material from August 2023 addresses website/CMS development, technology consulting, SaaS administration, digital operations, and technology education. Such planning material confirms business positioning, **not** completion of every contemplated service.
