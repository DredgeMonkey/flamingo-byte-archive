**Flamingo Byte** was an independent technology consulting and digital-services business founded by Spencer Armand, with professional activity in **2023–2024**. This repository documents the former business, selected historical work, and surviving evidence. It is **not** an active business website, a solicitation, or a claim that every proposed service or project reached completion.

## Work and professional context

The business involved technology consulting, website/CMS development, business SaaS and systems support, technical writing, and technology-literacy publishing.

Representative activity included:

- **Marine Restoration Foundation:** A relationship beginning **before** Flamingo Byte, with later pro bono proposal development, research coordination, and laboratory-collaboration documentation associated with the LLC. Proposals should not be confused with completed restoration or research outcomes.
- **Independent medical-practice consulting:** A longstanding relationship involving healthcare websites, systems support, and work associated with a transition to CharmHealth. Some later work occurred during the Flamingo Byte period; earlier work was not performed under the LLC.
- **Technology literacy:** Historical site content, business-technology writing, and educational material.

These activities could overlap with other employment, independent engagements, and Florida Intracoastal Training. This archive does **not** imply an exclusive full-time chronology or a formal legal succession to CogniVibe.
